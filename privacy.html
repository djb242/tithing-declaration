Tithing Declaration

Privacy

In short: your ward's information stays on your device and in your own Google Drive or OneDrive. Nobody else, including the people who made this app, can see it.

What the app stores, and where

  • The families you add, their contact details and notes, the appointments, and a log of when you reached out. All of it is kept in this browser's storage on your device.
  • If you turn on sync, the same information is saved as one file in your own Google Drive or OneDrive. That file belongs to you, and you can delete it any time.
  • There is no server. The app is a set of files your browser downloads, and it then talks only to Google or Microsoft, directly from your device, using your sign-in.

What it asks Google or Microsoft for

  • Google Drive (drive.file): only the file this app creates, or one you pick that someone shared with you. It cannot see your other files.
  • OneDrive (Files.ReadWrite.All): to save your list file and to open one someone shared with you. Microsoft offers no narrower permission that reaches shared files. The app only ever opens files named "Tithing Declaration list".
  • Calendar (read only): only if you connect one, to see bookings and mark those families scheduled. Events are read on your device. Nothing is written to your calendar.

What it never does

  • It never sends your information to the app's makers or to anyone else.
  • It uses no AI of any kind.
  • It never sends a text, email or call for you. It opens your own messaging app with the words filled in, and you choose whether to send.
  • It has no ads, analytics or tracking.

Sharing

If you share your list, the people you share with can see and edit everything in it. Share only with people who would see this information anyway, and follow your leaders' guidance on member information.

Removing your information

Settings → Erase removes everything from that device. To remove the synced copy, delete the "Tithing Declaration list" file from your Drive or OneDrive. To remove the app's access, visit your Google account's "Third-party connections" or Microsoft's "Apps and services" page.
